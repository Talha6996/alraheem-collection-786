## Chosen approach: Heritage Atelier

### Design Movement

Contemporary South Asian editorial retail: a refined storefront that combines the material warmth of a small atelier with the discoverability and category rhythm of a large online marketplace.

### Core Principles

1. Use **asymmetric editorial composition** rather than generic centered sales blocks.
2. Balance deep navy authority with antique-ivory breathing room and a restrained rust accent drawn from the supplied mark.
3. Treat merchandise photography as the main graphic material, with minimal product cards and generous negative space.
4. Make promotions feel collected and considered, not loud: small caps, fine rules, warm surfaces, and deliberately placed labels.

### Color Philosophy

The palette is grounded in **deep indigo** as an expression of trust, craft, and the logo’s strongest color; **aged ivory** creates softness and makes products feel touchable; **terracotta red** appears sparingly to echo the diagonal monogram bar and direct attention. Pale stone and muted sage are supporting tones for quiet contrast. The site remains light overall, with dark color used strategically to frame commerce moments.

### Layout Paradigm

The homepage is designed as a sequence of editorial slabs: a slim offer strip, a split hero where headline and image carry equal weight, an offset category rail, and horizontally scrollable product stories. Product discovery mirrors Bagallery’s high-level structure—utility header, search, category navigation, hero campaigns, product collections, branded services, and footer—while all branding, composition, copy, and styling are original.

### Signature Elements

1. Fine double-line framing and circular stamp motifs that recall the supplied logo’s seal shape.
2. Rust-red diagonal strokes as section connectors and active-state markers.
3. Navy product labels set in compact uppercase with small ivory chips.

### Interaction Philosophy

Browsing should feel like opening a considered catalogue. Hovering reveals product actions softly; category selections bring the matching products into focus; search, saved items, and cart always give immediate clear feedback. Buttons have a light tactile press and no distracting animation.

### Animation

Use 160–240ms custom ease-out transitions. Hero copy and collection cards may make a single low-distance opacity-and-translate entrance on load, staggered by 50ms. Product images lift 4px on hover while a rust underline expands. Respect reduced-motion preferences by disabling nonessential transitions.

### Typography System

Use **DM Serif Display** for elegant editorial headlines, **Manrope** for functional sans-serif UI and body text, and Manrope uppercase with generous tracking for metadata. Headlines have expressive contrast and compact lines; navigation and product details stay crisp, small, and highly legible.

### Brand Essence

**ALRAHEEM COLLECTION 786 is a carefully composed online edit of everyday fashion, beauty, accessories, and gifting for shoppers who value distinctive finds and direct service.**

Personality: **assured, warm, collected**.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are composed and direct, while CTAs make a specific invitation. Avoid generic online retail language and exaggerated promises.

Example lines:

> Your everyday, arranged with intention.

> Find the piece that finishes the look.

### Wordmark & Logo

The supplied circular **AR** seal is the primary graphic mark. It appears clearly in the header and footer, paired with a spaced, uppercase ALRAHEEM COLLECTION 786 wordmark in Manrope. The diagonal red monogram bar becomes a repeatable graphic accent.

### Signature Brand Color

**Alraheem Indigo — #123F72**

## Style Decisions

- Product imagery follows one curated atelier shoot direction: warm neutral settings, tactile material, indigo/ivory/rust harmony, and no novelty graphics or high-saturation stock treatments.
- Each collection surface carries a Heritage Atelier cue, including fine-line frames, edition labels, seal language, rust diagonal strokes, or editorial notes.
- Promotional color remains disciplined: Alraheem Indigo and aged ivory are structural; terracotta/rust is the recurring attention accent; bright pink and neon treatments are excluded.

## Verification Log

Desktop and mobile visual checks confirm the split hero, collection filters, product cards, gift composition, newsletter, and footer adapt without visible overflow. The mobile layout uses a compact header, accessible off-canvas navigation, horizontally browsable category cards, and a two-column product edit.
