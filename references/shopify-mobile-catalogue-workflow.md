# Shopify Mobile Product Upload Workflow

The seven requested categories now exist in Shopify as **manual collections**. During product upload, select the appropriate collection yourself in the product’s **Collections** area.

| Storefront category | Select this manual Shopify collection | Product type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| JEWELLERY | `JEWELLERY` | Optional: `Jewellery` |
| HANDBAGS | `HANDBAGS` | Optional: `Handbags` |
| LADIES SUIT | `LADIES SUIT` | Optional: `Ladies Suit` |
| MENS SUIT | `MENS SUIT` | Optional: `Mens Suit` |
| BRANDED KARA | `BRANDED KARA` | Optional: `Branded Kara` |
| BRIDAL SETS | `BRIDAL SETS` | Optional: `Bridal Sets` |
| MENS BRACELET | `MENS BRACELET` | Optional: `Mens Bracelet` |

In the Shopify mobile app, open **Products**, tap **+**, and add the product title, media, description, price, and stock details. In the product’s organization area, open **Collections** and select the matching collection from the table. This manual collection selection now controls the website category; **Product type** is optional and may be used for your internal Shopify organization. Save the product as **Active** and make it available to the **Manus** sales channel.

Shopify Admin verification confirmed that the product editor’s **Product organization → Collections** control displays `BRANDED KARA` as a selectable manual collection for White Stone Kara. All seven category collections are published to the **Manus** channel, and the `BRANDED KARA` storefront filter was verified to return its two manually assigned products.

The Shopify Product category field can still be selected for Shopify’s standardized taxonomy, but it does not control this website’s seven category links. Do not use a tag or a different spelling as a substitute for the matching manual collection.

Shopify documents that a manual collection lets merchants choose products directly; products remain in a manual collection until removed. [1]
